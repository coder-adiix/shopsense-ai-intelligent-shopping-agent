# 🛍️ ShopSense AI – Intelligent Shopping Agent

**ShopSense AI** is an AI-powered intelligent shopping assistant designed to help users make smarter and faster purchasing decisions.

Instead of manually searching through hundreds of products, users can describe what they need, specify their budget and priorities, and get a focused product shortlist based on relevant factors such as price, features, ratings, reviews, and overall value.

## 🚀 Features

* 🤖 **AI-Powered Shopping Assistant**

  * Understands user requirements and shopping preferences.

* 🧠 **Personalized Recommendations**

  * Considers budget, preferences, urgency, and priorities.

* 📊 **Smart Product Comparison**

  * Compares product price, ratings, features, and value.

* ⭐ **Review Intelligence**

  * Helps identify useful product advantages, disadvantages, and customer concerns.

* 💰 **Value-Based Selection**

  * Goes beyond product price to identify better overall value.

* ⚡ **Fast Shortlisting**

  * Converts many product choices into a focused shortlist.

* 🌱 **Context-Aware Recommendations**

  * Can consider factors such as sustainability, delivery urgency, and long-term usefulness.

* 🔎 **Interactive Product Search**

  * Users can search the sample product catalog using product names, categories, or budgets.

## 💡 Problem Statement

Online shopping provides users with a huge number of products and choices. Comparing prices, features, ratings, reviews, and value across multiple products can be time-consuming and confusing.

ShopSense AI aims to simplify this process by acting as an intelligent shopping companion that helps users identify products that best match their requirements.

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective of ShopSense AI is to:

1. Reduce the time required to search for products.
2. Understand individual shopping requirements.
3. Compare products based on important factors.
4. Provide personalized recommendations.
5. Help users make confident purchasing decisions.

## ⚙️ How It Works

The system follows a simple four-step process:

### 1. Tell Us

The user describes the required product, budget, and priorities.

### 2. AI Analyzes

The system evaluates available products and relevant signals against the user's requirements.

### 3. Compare

Products are presented in a clear shortlist with match scores and important information.

### 4. Decide

The user can select a suitable product using the summarized information.
